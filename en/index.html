02 — Capabilities

Seven areas of expertise.

Seven areas of expertise behind every STMetal project.

  • 01

    Industrial piping

    Fabrication, preparation and installation of piping systems for industrial applications.

    • Pipe fabrication. Fabrication and preparation of components for industrial systems.
    • Industrial installation. Assembly and installation of piping in industrial settings.
    • Welding and control. Welding, dimensional control and technical preparation.

  • 02

    Special materials

    Experience preparing, fabricating and welding materials for technically demanding industrial applications.

    • Super Duplex. Applications needing high corrosion resistance.
    • P22 · P91 · P92. Materials for high thermal and mechanical demands.
    • Technical welding. Carried out to the project’s technical requirements.

  • 03

    Qualified industrial welding

    Preparation and execution of welds for industrial applications, including technically demanding materials.

    • ASME IX. Qualification procedures and requirements.
    • ISO 9606. Welder qualification.

  • 04

    Pipe prefabrication

    From cutting to final preparation for installation on site. Controlled prefabrication to streamline execution and shorten time on site.

    • Cutting and preparation. Preparation of pipes and components.
    • Assembly. Assembly and preparation of spools.
    • Technical welding. Carried out to technical requirements.
    • Final check. Inspection and preparation for installation.

  • 05

    Industrial boilermaking

    Custom-made metal components for a range of industrial applications.

    • Made to measure. Metal components produced to suit the application.
    • Forming and welding. Preparation, forming and joining of components.
    • Industrial installation. Assembly and installation of units on site.
    • Inspection and finishing. Control, finishing and preparation for installation.

  • 06

    Steel structures

    Fabrication and erection of structures for industrial and civil applications.

    • 01 · Cutting and preparation
    • 02 · Fabrication. Assembly · welding.
    • 03 · Dimensional control

  • 07

    Industrial installation

    Assembly and installation of components and structures in industrial settings, to each project’s technical and safety requirements.

    • Technical preparation
    • Industrial installation
    • Installation and adjustment
